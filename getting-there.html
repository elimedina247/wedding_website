Getting There

Fly into Belize City, then a short hop to the island.

  1. 1

    Fly into Belize City

    Book a flight to Philip S. W. Goldson International Airport (BZE) in Belize City. Several major U.S. airports fly there direct.

  2. 2

    Hop over to Ambergris Caye

    From Belize City, pick how you'd like to get to San Pedro, the island's town.

    By air

    Puddle jumper

    About 15–20 minutes

    Maya Island Air flies small planes from the international airport straight to San Pedro, with great views of the reef on the way in.

    Small planes have tighter luggage limits, so check before you pack.

    • Maya Island Air Book a flight Schedule

    By sea

    Ferry (water taxi)

    About 90 minutes, plus a cab

    Take a taxi from the airport to the water taxi terminal in Belize City (about 25 minutes), then ride the ferry to San Pedro. It usually stops at Caye Caulker along the way.

    The budget-friendly option. Two companies run this route:

    • San Pedro Belize Express Book Schedule
    • Caribbean Sprinter Book Schedule
  3. 3

    On to Matachica

    Matachica is about five miles north of San Pedro, roughly 20 minutes by boat. Once your travel is booked, contact the resort with your arrival details to arrange your boat from town.

Good to know

  • Flights and boats to the island stop running in the early evening, so try to land in Belize City by mid-afternoon.
  • You'll need a valid passport to enter Belize.
